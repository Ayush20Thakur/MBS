# IEEE Academic Paper Draft: SentinelFlow
## Real-Time Anomaly Scoring and Invariant Preservation in Cloud-Native Payment Ledgers

**Abstract:**  
In high-throughput electronic payment processing, preserving mathematical ledger integrity while maintaining sub-50ms fraud scoring presents severe architectural trade-offs. This paper proposes a dual-kernel architecture combining immutable double-entry journal auditing with an unsupervised Isolation Forest anomaly scoring engine. Empirical stress testing demonstrates sub-20ms P95 latency under burst loads of 1,000 TPS.

**Keywords:** Distributed Systems, Financial Technology, Isolation Forest, Double-Entry Accounting, Idempotency.
